The MDPI articles can be stored in a directory named as "MDPI_Articles"

Some clarifications:

1) Epub Articles 1...N --> The MDPI downloaded articles in epub format for a journal
2) Downloaded_Articles.txt --> A list of all successfully downloaded articles
3) MDPI_{Journal}_Abstracts.json --> A dictionary containing the titles, keywords, and abstracts of the journal downloaded articles
4) MDPI_{Journal}_Augmented_Content.json --> A dictionary containing the titles, keywords, and (abstracts + text content from the full article) of the journal downloaded articles
5) Installation_Results.txt --> Information regarding the installation process of the MDPI articles for all journals
